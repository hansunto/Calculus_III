    1.

    Find the center and radius of the sphere given by
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} x^2+y^2+z^2-10x+3y+5z+\frac{47}{2}=0. \end{equation*}
    Solution.
    Group the terms involving each variable:
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} (x^2-10x)+(y^2+3y)+(z^2+5z) =-\frac{47}{2}. \end{equation*}
    Complete the square for each variable:
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} x^2-10x=(x-5)^2-25. \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} y^2+3y=\left(y+\frac{3}{2}\right)^2-\frac{9}{4}. \end{equation*}
    \begin{equation*} z^2+5z=\left(z+\frac{5}{2}\right)^2-\frac{25}{4}. \end{equation*}
    Substitute these expressions into the equation:
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} (x-5)^2+ \left(y+\frac{3}{2}\right)^2+ \left(z+\frac{5}{2}\right)^2 = -\frac{47}{2}+25+\frac{9}{4}+\frac{25}{4}. \end{equation*}
    Simplify the right-hand side:
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} -\frac{47}{2}+25+\frac{9}{4}+\frac{25}{4}=10. \end{equation*}
    Therefore, the sphere is in standard form:
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} (x-5)^2+ \left(y+\frac{3}{2}\right)^2+ \left(z+\frac{5}{2}\right)^2 =10. \end{equation*}
    The standard equation of a sphere is
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} (x-h)^2+(y-k)^2+(z-\ell)^2=r^2. \end{equation*}
    Thus, the center is
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \left(5,-\frac{3}{2},-\frac{5}{2}\right) \end{equation*}
    and the radius is
    🔗
    \begin{equation*} \sqrt{10}. \end{equation*}
    Therefore, Center: \(\left(5,-\frac{3}{2},-\frac{5}{2}\right)\) and radius: \(\sqrt{10}\text{.}\)
